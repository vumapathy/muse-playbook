# 2026-11-23 — Phone task lessons (sanitized, public)

One automated phone call today: an appointment cancellation at an independent optometrist, completed on the first attempt in 34 seconds. No browser-based phone calls today. The cancellation had failed twice the previous day because the office was closed; the scheduled Monday-morning callback closed it.

## Cancellation calls

1. **Schedule the callback when a call fails on hours.** The previous day's calls established the office was closed on Sundays; the review scheduled a Monday-morning callback, and it completed on the first attempt. When a call fails on hours, the follow-up slot is part of the outcome — schedule it immediately and the task closes cheaply.
2. **Ask for explicit confirmation by name, date, and time.** "Could you please confirm that this appointment ... has been cancelled?" got a clean "Yes, cancelled." Don't assume a polite "Thank you" means the action is done — ask for the confirmation and let the other side say it.
3. **Put the complete ask in the first sentence.** Name, date, time, and doctor up front meant no back-and-forth was needed. For a cancellation call, everything the other side needs to act should be in the opener.
4. **Never volunteer personal identifiers.** No identifiers were requested on this call and none were offered. If they are requested, decline and accept the general-answer outcome rather than pushing past the block.
