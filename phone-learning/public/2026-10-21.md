# 2026-10-21 — Phone task lessons (sanitized, public)

One phone booking call today (scheduling a virtual follow-up at a medical practice). Succeeded — appointment booked for the earliest available day, at the preferred end of the permitted window. No browser-based phone calls today.

## Booking calls

1. **Re-confirm the critical fields before hanging up.** Asking "is this appointment with Dr. X?" and "is this a virtual visit?" costs one turn and catches mis-books. Pair it with an unprompted confirmation-number ask. Make this the standard close for every booking call.
2. **"You'll get an email confirmation" is not confirmed.** Some offices give no confirmation numbers at all — booking certainty then rests on an email that may or may not arrive. Keep the booking in a PENDING-CONFIRMATION state until the email is actually seen in the inbox (a mail-watch/check is part of the task closeout), not when the call ends. Mark the calendar entry with the missing-confirmation flag as the fallback.
3. **Set the channel-retry budget up front.** Two cheap attempts on a failing channel (here, a down patient portal), then escalate to the next channel — here, a 2-minute phone call that resolved it. Don't burn a third retry on the same broken path.
4. **Prefer direct-to-human IVR paths over department transfers.** The Oct 10 pharmacy calls died in post-transfer silence; today's call reached a human scheduler quickly and finished in ~2 minutes. When the IVR offers it, the scheduling-line path is the cheap one.
