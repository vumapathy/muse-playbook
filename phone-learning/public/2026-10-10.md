# 2026-10-10 — Phone task lessons (sanitized, public)

Two pharmacy store-hours calls today (checking weekend hours and whether the photo counter keeps different hours than the main store). One failed, one partially succeeded. No browser-based phone calls today.

## Pharmacy-chain IVRs

1. **Transfers are the failure point, not the IVR.** Both calls navigated the IVR menus fine and both died in post-transfer silence. After a transfer, hold the line — live-department transfers routinely take 60–120+ seconds of silence/hold. Giving up after a few quiet cycles and hanging up wastes the whole call. Wait at least 2 minutes of quiet after "transferring you now" before concluding failure; announce you'll hold rather than repeating the question into silence.
2. **One input per IVR prompt.** If you pressed a keypad button, don't also speak the answer when the menu re-prompts. Double input wastes turns and can confuse menu state machines.
3. **Fix opener overlap in the call config.** First sentences keep getting talked over by the greeting and cut off mid-word. Keep the opener to one short sentence and add a brief pause before speaking (or raise the interruption threshold) — noting it in the review isn't enough; it recurs across days.
4. **Sequence questions; don't bundle a department-specific question with a general one.** A general IVR answers general questions fine; bundling "and does the photo counter close earlier" into the same turn is what triggered a transfer the agent couldn't survive. Get the general answer first, then ask for the department as a separate step — or ask to be connected to the department directly instead of asking the general agent a question it can't answer.
5. **Retry fast, but change the failing step on the retry.** A 5-minute-later retry got partial info — the fast cheap retry pattern works — but it repeated the same transfer-death on the remaining questions. A retry should adjust strategy, not just redial.
6. **Pre-call check: what is already known?** One call re-asked for hours that were already established from earlier research. Lead with the genuinely missing facts.
